# Breakout Sessions — Presenter Resources

## About these sessions

Breakout sessions are 35-minute technical presentations, including Q&A, designed to help attendees understand and explore GitHub's latest capabilities through practical examples and demonstrations.

Each session includes presentation materials and speaker guidance to help presenters confidently deliver the content.

## Getting started

Navigate to the folder for your assigned breakout session. Each session folder contains the resources you need to prepare and deliver your talk.

1. **Read the session README** — Start here for an overview of the session, key messages, and available resources.
2. **Review the presentation deck** — Familiarise yourself with the slides and presenter notes.
3. **Use the Speaker Guide** — Follow the suggested session flow, key talking points, and demonstration guidance.
4. **Review supporting materials** — Watch the recorded walkthrough or consult the transcript, where available, for additional context.

## Presenter guidelines

- **30 minutes total**, including audience Q&A. Aim for approximately 25 minutes of content and 5 minutes of questions.
- Use the Speaker Guide to understand the session narrative and important messages.
- Deliver the presentation in your own style rather than reading directly from the slides.
- Rehearse any demonstrations and prepare a fallback where necessary.
- Keep the session focused, engaging, and relevant to the audience.
- Leave sufficient time for questions and discussion.

## Before your session

- [ ] Read your session README and Speaker Guide.
- [ ] Review and rehearse the presentation.
- [ ] Familiarise yourself with any demonstrations or supporting recordings.
- [ ] Verify access to any required tools or environments.
- [ ] Confirm you can deliver the session within 30 minutes, including Q&A.

**Remember:** These sessions are designed to be easily reproduced by different presenters. The resources provide the structure and guidance, but bring your own experience and personality to the delivery.
