## What the host is responsible for

The host connects the day's content and the attendee experience. Your job is to welcome the audience, explain what is happening and where to go, introduce speakers, keep the event on time, promote the hands-on experiences, and guide attendees through Ship & Tell and the reception.

Before delivering, coordinate with the local event lead, stage/AV team, speaker manager, hands-on station teams, and Ship & Tell submissions lead. Roles can be combined at smaller events; confirm who is doing what rather than assuming someone else owns a handoff.

## Suggested host delivery flow

### 1. Opening and welcome

- Have the approved opening media and welcome slide ready before the event begins.
- Welcome the audience, introduce yourself, and thank attendees for joining their local Universe on the Road event.
- Cover practical information: Wi-Fi, venue facilities, photography, partners, and the agenda. Keep housekeeping concise and useful.
- Preview the afternoon's three tracks and explain where participants can find the Demo & Donuts / hands-on areas.
- **Promote Ship & Tell during the opening** so attendees have time to create and submit a project. Show Slide 7 and tell them to speak to the team at a hands-on station.
- Introduce the opening keynote speaker and hand over on time.

### 2. Morning handoffs

- Confirm the next speaker is ready before returning to the stage.
- Introduce the product updates session at **10:20** using the approved speaker name and title.
- At **10:50**, announce the break and the **11:15** return time.
- At **11:15**, introduce the customer stories and Q&A participants. Explain how audience questions will be collected.
- At **12:00**, announce lunch, its location, and the **13:00** return for the afternoon tracks. Remind attendees that Ship & Tell projects must be submitted by **15:00**.

### 3. Afternoon orientation

- Show the afternoon agenda (Slide 6) where helpful, and clearly identify the three tracks, their rooms/areas, and the session start times.
- Explain that Track 3 is the Demo & Donuts / Terminal Maker's Space experience.
- Remind attendees to talk to the hands-on station teams about a Ship & Tell nomination.
- Confirm when attendees should return to the main stage for the **15:35** Ship & Tell finale.

### 4. Ship & Tell finale and awards

Follow the [Ship & Tell City Execution Summary](https://github.com/microsoft/universeontheroad2026-resources/blob/main/sessions/ship-and-tell/README.md). The outline below is a host-focused summary; the linked guide remains the source for local execution.

**Collect submission before 15:00**

- Hands-on station teams encourage participants to build something with the GitHub Copilot app: a game, a useful app, or a solution to a technical challenge.
- Station teams collect or nominate projects for consideration; they may nominate more than one.
- The local submissions lead coordinates the shortlist and gathers screenshots, recordings, links, or demo access.
- The host reminds attendees that the **submission cutoff is 15:00**.

**15:00–15:35 — prepare the Final Five**

- Close submissions and select **five projects** for the main-stage showcase.
- Confirm each participant's name, project title, attendance, and consent to present.
- Prepare project visuals in the correct order. Test every link, recording, and live demo path.
- Load and test the local voting method and a backup. Decide who operates the timer and voting tool.

**15:35–16:00 — stage finale**

**Voting:** The [execution guide](https://github.com/microsoft/universeontheroad2026-resources/blob/main/sessions/ship-and-tell/README.md) recommends a digital tool such as [Slido](https://www.slido.com/) and also describes an applause-based fallback. Choose the local method in advance. If using digital voting, replace Slide 8's placeholder graphic with a working local QR, test audience capacity, and verify both rounds. Prepare a clear fallback if Wi-Fi or polling fails.

### 5. Closing and reception

- Encourage everyone to complete the event feedback survey (Slide 13).
- Thank speakers, partners, the local event team, participants, and attendees.
- Announce the **16:00–17:00 reception** and tell attendees where to find drinks, food, partners, and GitHub experts.


## Speaker task list — complete before your event

Use this as the city-specific sign-off checklist. Tasks refer to the uploaded host template*

### Required deck changes

- [ ] **Slide 3 — City:** Replace `CITY` with the correct location. Check spelling and any local branding requirements.
- [ ] **Slide 3 — Template cleanup:** Remove the stray template-instructions text if it is visible in the final file.
- [ ] **Slide 4 — Host details:** Confirm the host's name, title, portrait, and pronouncing instructions. The current example shows **April Yoho**; replace it for other hosts.
- [ ] **Slides 5–6 — Agenda:** Verify morning and afternoon times, track names, session titles, and room assignments against the final local programme. Replace the Slide 6 image if anything differs.
- [ ] **Slide 9 — Wi-Fi:** Replace `[NETWORK NAME]` and `[PASSWORD OR SIGN-IN INSTRUCTIONS]`. Test with a guest device; verify whether a captive portal, voucher, or QR login is needed.
- [ ] **Slide 11 — Partners:** Replace `[PARTNER 1]`, `[PARTNER 2]`, and `[PARTNER 3]` with confirmed local partners. Rework the layout if the actual number differs; check approved spelling and logo use.
- [ ] **Slide 8 — Voting:** Replace the **ADD LOCAL VOTING QR** placeholder with the real polling QR, or adapt the slide to explain the agreed applause method. Test from a phone.
- [ ] **Slide 13 — Feedback:** Confirm that the existing QR goes to the correct survey for this city/event; replace it if necessary.
- [ ] **Slide 12 — Keynote:** Confirm the transition slide, keynote speaker's name/title, and handoff timing.
- [ ] **Core resources — Host deck link:** Upload or identify the approved local PowerPoint and insert its link in the Core resources table above.


### Ship & Tell setup and rehearsal

- [ ] Assign the station teams, submissions lead, stage host, AV operator, and marketing/session support.
- [ ] Agree how projects are submitted or nominated, who receives submissions, and the **15:00 cutoff**.
- [ ] Confirm the five-project selection process, contact details, and format for project previews.
- [ ] Prepare the Final Five showcase materials and decide how unavailable presenters will be handled.
- [ ] Agree the **two-round** voting method and test it with a full dummy vote. Reset Round 2 voting data.
- [ ] Confirm that **each project gets 90 seconds** and assign a visible timekeeper.
- [ ] Confirm whether first-, second-, and third-place prizes exist, who presents them, and where they are kept.
- [ ] Rehearse winner announcements, stage movement, photography, and the transition to the reception.
- [ ] Prepare a no-internet / polling-failure backup and a screenshot/video fallback for live project demos.

### Technical and final checks

- [ ] Check all QRs from the back of the room and confirm they lead to the intended event-specific pages.
- [ ] Test the slide clicker, stage confidence monitor, microphone, any video audio, and the presenter view.
- [ ] Confirm who advances slides and where the host should enter/exit the stage.
- [ ] Remove unused slides, test assets, or duplicate endings; confirm the intended order for live delivery.
- [ ] Save a local/offline PowerPoint backup and a PDF fallback (noting that a PDF will not play embedded media).
- [ ] Complete one full run-through with the local production team and check timing against the approved show schedule.
